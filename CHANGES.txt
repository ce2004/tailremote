1.7.0
Works with 1.6, 1.5 and 1.4, so the two PCs can update one at a time.
Changed: the sound reacts to the connection much faster. It steps down within a fifth of a second of trouble, two steps at once when it is bad, and climbs back after a second and a half of clean sound, one step a second. If climbing back brings the trouble straight back, it waits longer before trying again.
New feature: in Host mode, Capture sound from (Alt A) chooses which output the other PCs hear, or Windows default. Changing it while hosting switches straight away. The service uses it too after Apply settings to the service.

1.6.0
Works with 1.5 and 1.4, so the two PCs can update one at a time.
Changed: on a slow or struggling connection, the sound now steps down in sample rate instead of detail: 32, 24, 16, 11 and finally 8 kHz, which is phone quality and still perfectly clear for speech. Every step is lossless at its own rate. At 8 kHz it uses a small fraction of the data. It climbs back to full quality after 10 good seconds, and the window title says when the sound is reduced.

1.5.0
Works with 1.4, so the two PCs can update one at a time.
New feature: sound uses about a fifth less data again, and is still exactly lossless.
New feature: on a slow or struggling connection, TailRemote drops the finest detail one step at a time (down to 12-bit) instead of letting the sound lag, and goes back to lossless after 10 good seconds. The window title says when it has.
Safer: TailRemote cannot be frozen by someone flooding it with logins, by a PC that stops responding, or by many listeners. After 5 wrong passwords, that address is refused for a minute.
Faster: less work and less memory churn while sound plays.

1.4.1
Works with 1.4.0, so the two PCs can update one at a time.
New feature: while hosting, closing the window hides TailRemote in the notification area, where it keeps hosting. Press Windows B to find it; its menu has Stop hosting and exit.
New feature: up to 100 people can listen at once.
Bug fixed: connecting or disconnecting cut all sound on the PC for about a second.
Bug fixed: the sound crackled, most of all from a busy cloud PC. Audio that arrived late was treated as silence; now only real silence is. When sound does run short, it fades instead of clicking.

1.4.0
Update both PCs: this version cannot connect to older ones.
New feature: Run as a Windows service, in Host mode. The remote PC can then be used at the lock screen, the sign-in screen and administrator prompts, even right after a restart, and Control Alt End sends Control Alt Delete.
New feature: Restart remote PC and reconnect. TailRemote reconnects by itself when the remote PC is back, and tells you.
New feature: Send files, both ways. Files arrive in Downloads, TailRemote, on the other PC, checked so nothing arrives damaged, and never overwrite anything.
New feature: the clipboard is shared. Copy text on one PC and paste it on the other. Turn it off with Share clipboard text.
New feature: a listen-only password. Up to 100 people can listen to the remote PC with it, without being able to control it.
New feature: Saved PCs. Save an address, port and password, and pick it from the list next time.
New feature: sound uses about half the data, with no change at all to how it sounds.
Changed: newer versions can add features without breaking older ones from now on.

1.3.0
Update both PCs: this version cannot connect to older ones.
New feature: everything TailRemote sends is encrypted, so you can connect with or without Tailscale, for example straight to a cloud PC's address.
New feature: Port editor opens and closes TailRemote's ports in Windows Firewall. It remembers every port TailRemote has used, so you can still close an old one after changing the port.
New feature: starting to host offers to open the port if Windows Firewall would keep other PCs out.
Changed: passwords need at least 5 characters, both for hosting and for connecting.
Changed: the Only accept Tailscale connections checkbox is gone. Anyone who knows the password can connect.
Changed: Start hosting when Windows starts no longer adds its own firewall rule, and removes the one older versions added. Use Port editor instead.

1.2.0
Update both PCs: this version cannot connect to older ones.
New feature: Remove audio device takes VB-Cable off the PC completely, after making your speakers the default again.
New feature: both audio buttons explain what they will do before they start, and they show in both modes. Before, Set up audio device only appeared in Host mode.
Safer: both PCs now prove they know the password, so your keys are never sent to a PC that does not know it. Wrong passwords are slowed down.
Bug fixed: after the host was quiet for a while, the sound could come back with almost half a second of extra delay.
Bug fixed: if the host's sound driver reset, its audio stayed silent until you reconnected.
Bug fixed: if your PC dropped off the network, keys you were holding could stay pressed on the host.
Bug fixed: holding Control Shift Enter flipped between the two PCs over and over.
Bug fixed: pressing Stop reconnecting could still connect a moment later.
Bug fixed: TailRemote could crash when closing while hosting.
Bug fixed: messages from the host, such as audio problems, are now spoken, not only written in the log.
Bug fixed: turning on Start hosting when Windows starts made the window stop responding while it worked.
Changed: the port is a plain edit box. A port that is not a number, or one other programs rely on (Remote Desktop, VNC, NVDA Remote, Tailscale), is refused with the reason. If another program is already using the port, hosting says so instead of starting.
Bug fixed: setting up the audio device also left VB-Cable's second output, CABLE In 16 Ch, switched on. It is turned off now, so only TailRemote is left. If you already set it up, press Set up audio device again to tidy it.
Bug fixed: setting up the audio device left its download in the Temp folder. It is deleted now.

1.1.0
New feature: Set up audio device, in Host mode, gives a PC with no sound card an output called TailRemote and makes it the default, so you can hear it. A progress window downloads VB-Cable, a free virtual audio device, checks it is genuine and opens its installer: press Install Driver and the rest happens by itself.
New feature: hosting on a PC with no sound output starts the audio device setup by itself.
New feature: if connecting fails, TailRemote says why: Tailscale is not signed in on this PC, the name was not found, the other PC is not hosting, or it is not answering.

1.0.0
First release: control another Windows PC over Tailscale with your whole keyboard, and hear everything it plays in 16-bit 44.1 kHz.
Control Shift Enter switches between the remote PC and this one.
The audio buffer sets itself: it measures your connection and keeps the delay as low as it can without breaking up.
A lost audio packet fades out smoothly instead of clicking.
If the connection drops, TailRemote reconnects on its own.
Check for updates downloads the new version and restarts where you left off.
